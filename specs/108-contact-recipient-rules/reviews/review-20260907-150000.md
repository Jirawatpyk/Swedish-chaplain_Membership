## Executive Summary

### ⚠️ APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS

**Merge blockers at the shipped flag state (`FEATURE_CONTACT_MARKETING_RECIPIENTS=false`): 0.**
**Conditions on the FLAG FLIP (T094): 4, one of them a 🔴.**

With the flag off the ceiling is 5,000 and the resolver runs `primary_only`, so prod behaves as it does today apart from the nine deliberately-unflagged changes enumerated in `quickstart.md` § Rollback matrix row C. What the review found is that **the audience ceiling was raised without the thing that pushes being able to deliver it** — and that the spec's own AMENDMENT asserted the opposite in its worked example. That is unreachable while the flag is off, so it gates T094, not this merge.

This was the branch's fifth review (round 1, round 2 ×12 reviewers, re-review, staff review). Each round found the previous round's fix landing one hop from where the class actually lived. This one is no exception: round 2 examined `SPLIT_THRESHOLD_RECIPIENTS` and concluded 5,001–10,000 was "not a gap" — a correct statement about **routing** that never checked the **wall clock**.
