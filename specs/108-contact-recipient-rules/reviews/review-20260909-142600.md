## Lessons agent 8 asked to have recorded

- **Widening a port's return type from `void` to a union requires sweeping every
  `vi.fn<…Promise<void>>` double — including files not in the diff.** The discriminant read turns

  Typing the two harnesses as `BuildAudienceTickDeps` makes the compiler enumerate the ports and
  retires the whole class.
