# Privacy Requirements Checklist (PDPA + GDPR): Member Portal — Approval Workflow for Member Changes

**Purpose**: Validate that the privacy requirements — lawful basis, purpose limitation, minimisation, data-subject rights, retention, cross-border, notification content — are complete, clear, consistent and measurable before `/speckit.tasks`. Formal gate; `pdpa-gdpr-compliance-officer` co-signs at `/speckit.review`.

**Feature**: [spec.md](../spec.md) · [plan.md](../plan.md) · [contracts/notifications-and-audit.md](../contracts/notifications-and-audit.md) · [research.md](../research.md)

## Requirement Completeness — lawful basis & purpose

- [ ] CHK001 Is the lawful basis for the new processing (holding proposed + superseded personal data of contacts until decided, and retaining it afterwards) stated **in the spec**, not only in the plan's Constitution Check? [Gap, Plan Constitution I names Art. 6(1)(b)/(f) + PDPA § 24(3)/(5); Spec § Assumptions is silent] → closed: FR-040
- [ ] CHK002 Is the purpose of each store defined and bounded — request rows (review + history), outbox rows (delivery only, ids only), audit rows (accountability, no values) — so purpose limitation is testable per store? [Completeness, Research R7, R8, Contract notifications §1–2]
- [ ] CHK003 Is the Art. 13/14 transparency duty addressed — are members told, at the point of submission, that their proposal is reviewed by chamber staff, emailed to them, and retained with the record for ≥ 5 years? [Gap, Spec §FR-010 defines the pending state but no notice text] → closed: FR-010 (Art. 13 / PDPA § 23 notice on the form)
- [ ] CHK004 Is the relationship to the rectification right (GDPR Art. 16 / PDPA § 35) stated: the request *is* the rectification channel, staff direct edit remains the immediate channel, and the one-month clock is backstopped by the FR-037 alert? [Consistency, Spec §FR-021, §FR-037, Clarifications Q1 rationale]

## Requirement Clarity — data minimisation

- [ ] CHK005 Is the rule "audit payloads carry ids, keys and outcomes — never field values or reason text" stated for all five events, including `reason_length` in place of the reason? [Clarity, Contract notifications §2, Spec §FR-025]
- [ ] CHK006 Is the rule "outbox `context_data` carries ids only; the diff is rendered at send time from the request rows" stated as a requirement so a sent notification row never holds names, phones or addresses? [Completeness, Research R8, Contract notifications §1]
- [ ] CHK007 Are the staff notification's personal-data contents justified as necessary (submitter name + role, field diffs) and is anything beyond that — e.g. the submitter's email, date of birth, other contacts — explicitly excluded? [Clarity, Spec §FR-011, Contract notifications §1]
- [ ] CHK008 Is the log-hygiene rule (pino: request ids, tenant, hashed user id — never values, reasons or emails) stated for the new use cases and routes? [Completeness, Contract notifications §4, `docs/observability.md` § 3]
- [ ] CHK009 Is the decision *reason* recognised as text that may itself contain personal data about the member (typed by staff), with a stated rule for its display audience, erasure and export? [Gap, Spec §FR-014 bounds length only] → closed: FR-014

## Requirement Completeness — data-subject rights

- [ ] CHK010 Is erasure specified at row level for the request store — values → sentinel, rows kept, pending closed as `withdrawn/erasure` by the system actor — and is a table-scoped guard test required so a new table cannot be forgotten? [Completeness, Spec §FR-030, §US4 AS6, Research R10]
- [ ] CHK011 Does erasure cover every copy: request rows, *queued-but-unsent* outbox rows for that member (staff notification, decision email), and audit rows (which by design hold no values)? [Coverage, Research R8, R10; Spec §FR-030 names only "the personal data inside requests"]
- [ ] CHK012 Is the data-subject access/portability export defined per **natural person** (the submitting contact) or per **member**, and is that scope consistent with FR-029's per-person visibility rule? [Consistency, Spec §FR-030 "scoped as in FR-029", Research R10 (member DSAR export via `requestDataExport`)]
- [ ] CHK013 Is the objection/restriction right addressed for staff-initiated processing of a rejected proposal — i.e. can a person ask that a rejected value be scrubbed before the 5-year floor, and who decides? [Gap, Spec §FR-030 retention floor only] → closed: FR-030 + § Out of Scope
- [ ] CHK014 Is the reviewer (a staff data subject) considered — their user id and display name are stored on every decision and shown to other staff; is retention/erasure of *staff* identity in history defined? [Gap, Data-model §1 `decided_by_user_id`] → closed: FR-026 + § Out of Scope

## Requirement Clarity — retention

- [ ] CHK015 Is retention stated with both a floor (≥ 5 years, aligned with the audit trail) and a trigger/upper bound (deleted with the member record, or never independently purged), so it is auditable rather than open-ended? [Clarity, Spec §FR-030, Research R10]
- [ ] CHK016 Is the outbox retention for the two new notification types stated (sent rows purged by the existing 90-day `outbox-purge`), and is it consistent with the ids-only content rule? [Consistency, Research R8; Spec is silent]
- [ ] CHK017 Is the rationale for *not* applying the 10-year tax-document retention class recorded, given company name and billing address feed future tax documents? [Assumption, Spec § Assumptions "Retention", Contract notifications §2]

## Requirement Completeness — cross-border & third parties

- [ ] CHK018 Is it stated that the staff notification (personal data of members) transits Resend and lands in staff mailboxes, and that the existing SCC / PDPA § 28 coverage for Resend applies to this new email type? [Assumption, Plan Constitution I "TLS/at-rest unchanged platform"; Spec is silent on the new email flows]
- [ ] CHK019 Are the record-of-processing (RoPA) and DPIA updates named as deliverables for this feature, given a new personal-data store and a new staff-facing disclosure? [Gap, Panel report § Unverified 8 "DPIA/RoPA update"; Spec/plan have no task hook] → closed: FR-040 + quickstart § 3 step 3 (RoPA)

## Requirement Consistency — scope of visibility

- [ ] CHK020 Do FR-029 (portal history), FR-028 (timeline) and FR-030 (export) agree on what one contact may see of another — and is the portal *timeline* specifically prevented from surfacing a colleague's own-field decision event? [Consistency, Spec §FR-028, §FR-029, Research R15]
- [ ] CHK021 Is the primary contact's visibility into a secondary's own-field request deliberately excluded (company accountability does not extend to a colleague's personal data), and is the rationale recorded? [Clarity, Spec §US4 AS4, Clarifications U4]
- [ ] CHK022 Is the manager/marketing read-only access to the queue (which shows personal data of contacts) consistent with those roles' existing member/contact PII access and with `members.pii_sensitive` fencing? [Consistency, Spec §FR-026, Contract admin "StaffChangeRequestView"]

## Edge Cases

- [ ] CHK023 Is behaviour defined when the submitting contact is removed or unlinked from the member while a request is pending (who is notified, is the request still decidable, does history still name them)? [Gap, Contract notifications §1 covers the email "recipient gone" only] → closed: FR-020 + § Edge Cases "Submitting contact removed"
- [ ] CHK024 Is behaviour defined when a *second* member erasure request arrives while change requests exist for both a primary and a secondary of the same member (per-person scrub vs member-wide scrub)? [Edge Case, Spec §FR-030] → closed: FR-030 (erasure is member-wide)
