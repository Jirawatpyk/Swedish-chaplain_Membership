## Requirement Completeness — abuse resistance & input handling

- [ ] CHK011 Is the 10-per-24 h cap fully specified — scope (per submitting person), what counts (every created request incl. replaced ones), source of truth (durable table, not a rate limiter), response (429 + `Retry-After`), and audit? [Completeness, Spec §FR-008, §SC-013, Research R9, Contract portal §2]
- [ ] CHK012 Are abuse scenarios beyond one person covered — a member with many portal contacts each submitting, or a tenant-wide burst — or is the per-person cap explicitly judged sufficient? [Gap, Spec §FR-008 has no per-member / per-tenant bound]
